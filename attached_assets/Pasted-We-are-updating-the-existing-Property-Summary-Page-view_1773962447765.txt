4. DEFINE ECONOMIC VACANCY (CRITICAL)

---

Economic Vacancy must be clearly defined and calculated consistently.

Definition:
Economic Vacancy (%) = 1 − (Effective Gross Income / Gross Potential Rent)

Where:

* Gross Potential Rent (GPR) = total possible rent at 100% occupancy (before vacancy and loss)
* Effective Gross Income (EGI) = actual collected income after vacancy, concessions, and loss to lease

Alternative interpretation (if GPR not available):
Economic Vacancy (%) = Vacancy Loss + Concessions + Bad Debt + Loss to Lease (as % of GPR)

Display:

* Show as a % (e.g., 7.2%)
* Subtitle: "Includes vacancy, concessions, and collection loss"

Important:

* Economic Vacancy is NOT the same as physical vacancy
* It reflects total income leakage, not just empty units

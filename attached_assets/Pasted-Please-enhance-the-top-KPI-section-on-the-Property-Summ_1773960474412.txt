GOAL

Create a 3-card financial KPI row at the top:

- Effective Gross Income (EGI)

- Net Operating Income (NOI)

--------------------------------------------------
KPI STRUCTURE (ALL THREE CARDS)
--------------------------------------------------

Each KPI card must follow this 3-row layout:

Row 1:
- Left: KPI label (EGI, Expenses, NOI)
- Right: status chip (Healthy / Watch / Risk)

Row 2:
- Large KPI value (primary focus)
- Include “LTM” label where applicable

Row 3:
- Change vs Budget
- Format:
  - ↑ $X,XXX vs Budget (positive)
  - ↓ ($X,XXX) vs Budget (negative)

--------------------------------------------------
DATA REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------

EGI:
- Value: Effective Gross Income (LTM)
- Change: EGI vs Budget

Expenses:
- Value: Total Operating Expenses (LTM)
- Change: Expenses vs Budget

NOI:
- Value: NOI (LTM)
- Change: NOI vs Budget

--------------------------------------------------
COLOR LOGIC
--------------------------------------------------

EGI:
- Positive variance vs budget = green
- Negative variance vs budget = red

NOI:
- Positive variance vs budget = green
- Negative variance vs budget = red

Expenses (IMPORTANT - inverse logic):
- Higher than budget = red (bad)
- Lower than budget = green (good)

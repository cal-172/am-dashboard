6. KEY DATES CARD REFINEMENT
--------------------------------------------------

Keep the Key Dates card, but improve urgency visibility.

Required statuses:
- Complete
- Upcoming
- Due Soon
- Overdue

Status logic recommendation:
- Complete: completed date exists
- Overdue: due date passed and no completion date
- Due Soon: due within 30 days and not complete
- Upcoming: future date outside due-soon window

Use color-coded chips:
- green = Complete
- yellow = Due Soon
- blue or neutral = Upcoming
- red = Overdue

Do not use spreadsheet-style checkboxes.
